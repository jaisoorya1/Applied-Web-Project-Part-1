Release Train Engineer

Description:

You will work closely with business stakeholders, technology teams, architects and vendors to drive delivery, manage dependencies and risks, and ensure initiatives progress effectively from planning through to deployment.

Salary:

$60 ~ $70 per hour

Read more

Key Responsibilities

Expand
  • Lead delivery initiatives from planning through execution, deployment and closure.
  • Work with business stakeholders to understand objectives, requirements and expected outcomes.
  • Partner with technology teams, architects and vendors to translate requirements into delivery plans.
  • Establish and manage delivery governance, reporting, RAID management and stakeholder communication.
  • Develop and maintain project plans, schedules, resource forecasts and delivery roadmaps.
  • Facilitate workshops, planning sessions, requirements discussions and decision-making forums.
  • Manage scope, dependencies, risks, issues and change requests.
  • Coordinate delivery across business, technology, testing, data and operational workstreams.
  • Drive testing, deployment planning, cutover and operational readiness activities.
  • Provide regular status updates to senior stakeholders, sponsors and steering committees.
  • Ensure initiatives are delivered on time and aligned with expected business outcomes.

Expand
  • SAFe and Agile Principles: Deep understanding of Lean-Agile mindset, Scrum
  • Flow & Systems Thinking: Ability to optimize value streams, understand queuing theory, and accelerate delivery flow.

Network Architect

Description:

Working within the Network Operations function, you will translate business, security and technology requirements into network capabilities, architecture patterns, standards, detailed designs and implementation roadmaps. You will also provide technical governance and design assurance across concurrent initiatives, and contribute directly to complex implementations where specialist expertise is required.

Salary:

$74 ~ $100 per hour

Read more

Key Responsibilities

Expand
  • Define and maintain enterprise network architecture, standards, patterns and roadmaps
  • Translate business, security and operational needs into end-to-end network solution designs
  • Lead the design and delivery of complex network initiatives and technical changes
  • Produce high-quality architecture and design artefacts (high-level and detailed designs, implementation guidance)
  • Provide architecture governance and technical assurance across projects and operational initiatives
  • Implement network security architecture aligned to Commonwealth frameworks (ISM, PSPF, Essential Eight)
  • Drive improvements to resilience, monitoring/observability, automation and operational supportability
  • Support capacity planning, lifecycle management and network modernisation
  • Engage and influence stakeholders across technical and non-technical groups

Essential Requirements

Expand
  • Networking Protocols: Deep knowledge of TCP/IP, BGP, OSPF, MPLS, VPNs, and software-defined networking
  • Hardware & Cloud: Familiarity with enterprise routers, switches, and firewalls from vendors like Cisco or Juniper, alongside cloud environments like AWS or Azure.
  • Security Architecture: Expertise in Zero Trust models, firewalls, encryption (IPSec, SSL/TLS), and compliance standards.
  • Automation: Proficiency in scripting or automation tools like Python, Bash, or Ansible.

While the primary focus is internal IT and cyber security, you will also have opportunities to support engineering consulting projects and contribute to innovative digital solutions.

Salary:

$100 ~ $125 per hour

Read more

Key Responsibilities

Expand
  • Monitor security events, investigate incidents and coordinate remediation.
  • Support compliance with recognised cyber security frameworks.
  • Develop and maintain IT and cyber security documentation.
  • Manage IT infrastructure, networks and technology vendors.
  • Implement and maintain cyber security controls including MFA, endpoint protection and email security.
  • Provide day-to-day IT support to employees.

  • Risk Management: Know how to test systems for vulnerabilities and respond to security breaches.
