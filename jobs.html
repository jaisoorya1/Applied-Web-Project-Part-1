Release Train Engineer

Description:

You will work closely with business stakeholders, technology teams, architects and vendors to drive delivery, manage dependencies and risks, and ensure initiatives progress effectively from planning through to deployment.

Salary:

$60 ~ $70 per hour

Read more

Key Responsibilities

Expand
  • Lead delivery initiatives from planning through execution, deployment and closure.
  • Work with business stakeholders to understand objectives, requirements and expected outcomes.
  • Partner with technology teams, architects and vendors to translate requirements into delivery plans.
  • Establish and manage delivery governance, reporting, RAID management and stakeholder communication.
  • Develop and maintain project plans, schedules, resource forecasts and delivery roadmaps.
  • Facilitate workshops, planning sessions, requirements discussions and decision-making forums.
  • Manage scope, dependencies, risks, issues and change requests.
  • Coordinate delivery across business, technology, testing, data and operational workstreams.
  • Drive testing, deployment planning, cutover and operational readiness activities.
  • Provide regular status updates to senior stakeholders, sponsors and steering committees.
  • Ensure initiatives are delivered on time and aligned with expected business outcomes.

Essential Requirements

Expand
  • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Business, Engineering, or a related field.
  • 3–7+ years of experience in Agile project management, program management, Scrum, or software delivery.
  • Practical experience working with Agile and SAFe (Scaled Agile Framework) methodologies.
  • Experience coordinating multiple Agile/Scrum teams within an Agile Release Train (ART).
  • Strong understanding of Scrum, Kanban, Lean, and SAFe principles.
  • Experience facilitating PI Planning, PI Planning preparation, System Demos, Inspect & Adapt, and Scrum of Scrums.
  • Strong program/project coordination and delivery management skills.
  • Ability to identify, track, and resolve cross-team dependencies, risks, issues, and impediments.
  • Excellent communication and stakeholder-management skills.
  • Ability to work effectively with Product Managers, Product Owners, Scrum Masters, Engineering Managers, Architects, and business stakeholders.
  • Strong facilitation, negotiation, conflict-resolution, and problem-solving abilities.
  • Experience using Agile delivery tools such as Jira, Azure DevOps, Confluence, or similar platforms.
  • Ability to monitor and communicate program-level progress, milestones, objectives, and delivery metrics.
  • Strong organizational and time-management skills.
  • Ability to operate effectively in a fast-paced, complex, and continuously changing environment.
  • Strong leadership skills with the ability to influence teams without direct authority.
  • Excellent written and verbal English communication skills.

Preferable Requirements

  1. SAFe Release Train Engineer (RTE) certification.
  2. Certified ScrumMaster (CSM), Professional Scrum Master (PSM), or equivalent Agile certification.
  3. SAFe Agilist (SAFe) certification.

  5. Experience in technology, software development, cloud, cybersecurity, or enterprise IT environments.
  6. Experience managing large-scale, distributed, or geographically dispersed teams.
  7. Familiarity with DevOps, CI/CD, cloud technologies, and software development lifecycle processes.
  8. Experience with Agile metrics such as velocity, predictability, throughput, cycle time, and flow metrics.

Network Architect

Description:

Working within the Network Operations function, you will translate business, security and technology requirements into network capabilities, architecture patterns, standards, detailed designs and implementation roadmaps. You will also provide technical governance and design assurance across concurrent initiatives, and contribute directly to complex implementations where specialist expertise is required.

Salary:

$74 ~ $100 per hour

Read more

Key Responsibilities

Expand
  • Define and maintain enterprise network architecture, standards, patterns and roadmaps
  • Translate business, security and operational needs into end-to-end network solution designs
  • Lead the design and delivery of complex network initiatives and technical changes
  • Produce high-quality architecture and design artefacts (high-level and detailed designs, implementation guidance)
  • Provide architecture governance and technical assurance across projects and operational initiatives
  • Implement network security architecture aligned to Commonwealth frameworks (ISM, PSPF, Essential Eight)
  • Drive improvements to resilience, monitoring/observability, automation and operational supportability
  • Support capacity planning, lifecycle management and network modernisation
  • Engage and influence stakeholders across technical and non-technical groups

Essential Requirements

Expand
  • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Networking, Engineering, or a related field.
  • 5+ years of professional experience in network engineering, network architecture, or infrastructure design.
  • Strong knowledge of enterprise network architecture and design principles.
  • Hands-on experience designing and implementing LAN, WAN, WLAN, and data center networks.
  • Strong knowledge of TCP/IP, DNS, DHCP, HTTP/HTTPS, VPN, NAT, and routing protocols.
  • Advanced knowledge of routing and switching technologies, including BGP, OSPF, EIGRP, VLANs, STP, and MPLS.
  • Experience with network security architecture, including firewalls, IDS/IPS, VPNs, network segmentation, and access controls.
  • Experience designing high-availability, resilient, and scalable network infrastructure.
  • Knowledge of cloud networking, including AWS, Microsoft Azure, or Google Cloud.
  • Experience with network technologies from vendors such as Cisco, Juniper, Palo Alto Networks, Fortinet, or equivalent.
  • Ability to develop network architecture diagrams, technical specifications, standards, and design documentation.
  • Experience conducting network performance, capacity, and security assessments.
  • Strong understanding of cybersecurity principles and network security best practices.
  • Experience with network monitoring, troubleshooting, and performance management.
  • Familiarity with SD-WAN, SDN, Zero Trust, and modern enterprise networking technologies.
  • Experience developing disaster recovery, business continuity, and network resilience strategies.
  • Strong analytical, troubleshooting, and problem-solving skills.
  • Excellent communication and stakeholder-management skills.
  • Ability to work with network engineers, security teams, cloud engineers, system administrators, and business stakeholders.
  • Ability to lead technical architecture decisions and provide technical guidance to engineering teams.

Preferable Requirements

Expand
  1. Cisco Certified Internetwork Expert (CCIE) or equivalent certification.
  2. Cisco Certified Network Professional (CCNP).
  3. Juniper Networks certification (JNCIP/JNCIE).
  4. Palo Alto Networks PCNSE or equivalent network-security certification.
  5. AWS Certified Advanced Networking – Specialty or equivalent cloud networking certification.
  6. Experience with network automation using Python, Ansible, Terraform, or similar tools.
  7. Knowledge of Infrastructure as Code (IaC) and DevOps practices.
  8. Experience designing networks for large enterprises, data centers, cloud environments, or cybersecurity organizations.
  9. Familiarity with regulatory and security frameworks such as ISO 27001, NIST, and PCI DSS.

Cybersecurity and IT Analyst

Description:

The Cyber Security & IT Systems Analyst is responsible for maintaining, securing and improving internal IT systems and cyber security environment. You will support our Microsoft 365 environment, cloud platforms, business systems and cyber security controls, ensuring our technology remains reliable, secure and efficient. While the primary focus is internal IT and cyber security, you will also have opportunities to support engineering consulting projects and contribute to innovative digital solutions.

Salary:

$100 ~ $125 per hour

Read more

Key Responsibilities

Expand
  • Monitor security events, investigate incidents and coordinate remediation.
  • Support compliance with recognised cyber security frameworks.
  • Develop and maintain IT and cyber security documentation.
  • Manage IT infrastructure, networks and technology vendors.
  • Implement and maintain cyber security controls including MFA, endpoint protection and email security.
  • Provide day-to-day IT support to employees.
  • Administer Microsoft 365, Teams, SharePoint, Entra ID and cloud services.
  • Maintain laptops, desktops, mobile devices and business applications.
  • Provide technical support to consulting projects when required.

Essential Requirements

Expand
  • Bachelor’s degree in Cybersecurity, Information Technology, Computer Science, Information Systems, or a related field.
  • 2–5 years of experience in cybersecurity, IT operations, security analysis, or a related technical role.
  • Strong understanding of cybersecurity principles, IT infrastructure, and information security best practices.
  • Knowledge of TCP/IP, DNS, DHCP, VPNs, firewalls, routing, switching, and network security.
  • Experience monitoring and analyzing security alerts, logs, and system events.
  • Familiarity with Security Information and Event Management (SIEM) platforms such as Microsoft Sentinel, Splunk, or QRadar.
  • Understanding of endpoint security, antivirus/EDR, vulnerability management, and patch management.
  • Ability to investigate and respond to security incidents, suspicious activity, and potential threats.
  • Knowledge of common cybersecurity threats, including phishing, malware, ransomware, credential attacks, and unauthorized access.
  • Familiarity with Windows and Linux operating systems.
  • Experience with Microsoft 365, Active Directory, Entra ID/Azure AD, or similar identity-management platforms.
  • Understanding of Identity and Access Management (IAM) and least-privilege principles.
  • Ability to conduct basic vulnerability assessments and security risk analysis.
  • Familiarity with security frameworks and standards such as NIST, ISO 27001, CIS Controls, or SOC 2.
  • Ability to document incidents, investigations, technical findings, and remediation activities.
  • Strong analytical, troubleshooting, and problem-solving skills.
  • Strong attention to detail and ability to identify unusual or potentially malicious activity.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills.
  • Ability to work collaboratively with IT, cybersecurity, network, cloud, and application teams.
  • Ability to prioritize multiple incidents, tasks, and security issues in a fast-paced environment.
  • Strong commitment to confidentiality, data protection, and information security.

Preferable Requirements

Expand
  1. CompTIA Security+, CySA+, or equivalent cybersecurity certification.
  2. CISSP, CISM, CEH, or GIAC certification.
  3. Experience with EDR/XDR, SOAR, vulnerability scanners, and network monitoring tools.
  4. Basic scripting or automation skills using PowerShell, Python, or Bash.
  5. Knowledge of cloud security across AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud.
  6. Experience with incident response and digital forensics.
  7. Familiarity with MITRE ATT&CK and threat-intelligence concepts.
  8. Experience supporting ISO 27001, NIST, SOC 2, PCI DSS, or other compliance requirements.
  9. Previous experience in a Security Operations Center (SOC) or enterprise IT environment.
